## Start with questions, not a full-site import
Write down your ten most common visitor questions. For each question, identify the public page that contains the answer. Improve that page if the answer is incomplete.

| Visitor question | Useful source | Check before importing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does this service include? | Service detail page | Scope and exclusions are explicit |
| Can I return this item? | Returns policy | Dates, exceptions, contact route are current |
| How do I book? | Booking information | Link works without a staff login |

## Review Crawler results
Inspect the imported content. Navigation, repeated footers, and out-of-date copies can obscure the actual answer. Do not assume a page was learned just because its URL was submitted. Check the result in your workspace and test a question from that page.

## Keep BrainVault focused
Use descriptive source names and remove contradictory versions. Assign a person to review policy and pricing changes. Never upload credentials, payment details, or customer records as public assistant knowledge.

## Confirm the result
Ask one direct question, one paraphrase, and one question your sources cannot answer. A successful result is accurate, understandable, and honest about missing information.
