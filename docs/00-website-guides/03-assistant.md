## Define one clear job
Write a short instruction describing the audience, the questions to help with, and what to do when information is missing.

> Help visitors understand our published services. Use the approved website content. Do not invent prices, availability, or guarantees. If the answer is missing, explain that and direct the visitor to our contact page.

Adapt this example to your business. Confirm the contact destination works before publishing.

## Make the tone fit your site
Prefer short, direct answers. Ask a clarifying question when a visitor's request is ambiguous. Avoid asking for sensitive information in chat.

## Set boundaries
Do not imply that the assistant can place orders, book appointments, access accounts, or transfer to a person unless that capability is configured and verified in your workspace. Linking to a booking page is different from completing a booking.

## Test the behavior
Ask about an unpublished price, a policy exception, and an unrelated topic. Check that the assistant follows its boundaries rather than guessing.
