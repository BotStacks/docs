Every customer can email [support@botstacks.ai](mailto:support@botstacks.ai) privately about account access, billing, privacy or security. Your plan determines the support available for routine product questions. See [support by plan](/common-tasks/usage-and-costs/track-costs#compare-plans) for the upcoming catalog. Existing customers keep their contracted support. Start with the [troubleshooting guide](/website-guides/maintenance) when a website assistant does not behave as expected.

## Include useful evidence
- The workspace and assistant name, plus your website platform.
- The public page URL and the time the problem occurred.
- What you expected and what happened instead.
- A redacted screenshot or a reproducible test question.
- An error or receipt reference if the product supplied one.

Remove visitor details, credentials, tokens, and full payment information. Never send a card security code or password.

## Match the problem to the next step
For an incorrect answer, include the approved public source. For a missing widget, include the published page rather than only the CMS editor. For a payment discrepancy, include the invoice reference and do not repeat the purchase while it is checked.

## Community and learning
[Follow the written setup walkthrough](/website-guides/start), browse the [BotStacks YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@BotStacksai/videos) for available videos, or use the community links on the [BotStacks website](https://www.botstacks.ai/) for general discussion. Guided setup is free on every plan. Community posts are not an appropriate place for private account or billing information. Support response times depend on your agreement; these docs do not promise a service-level commitment.
