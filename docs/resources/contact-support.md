Email [support@botstacks.ai](mailto:support@botstacks.ai) for account, billing, and product help. Start with the [troubleshooting guide](/website-guides/maintenance) when a website assistant does not behave as expected.

## Include useful evidence
- The workspace and assistant name, plus your website platform.
- The public page URL and the time the problem occurred.
- What you expected and what happened instead.
- A redacted screenshot or a reproducible test question.
- An error or receipt reference if the product supplied one.

Remove visitor details, credentials, tokens, and full payment information. Never send a card security code or password.

## Match the problem to the next step
For an incorrect answer, include the approved public source. For a missing widget, include the published page rather than only the CMS editor. For a payment discrepancy, include the invoice reference and do not repeat the purchase while it is checked.

## Community and learning
Use the community links on the [BotStacks website](https://www.botstacks.ai/) for general discussion. Community posts are not an appropriate place for private account or billing information. Support response times depend on your agreement; these docs do not promise a service-level commitment.
