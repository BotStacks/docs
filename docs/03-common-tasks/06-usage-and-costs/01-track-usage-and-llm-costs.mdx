# Billing and Pricing

Use Starter to try an assistant on one site. Choose Pro for one production site, Growth for several sites owned by one business, or Agency for separately managed clients. Monthly prices below are in USD.

:::important Purchasing availability
The four-plan catalog applies to new customers when paid checkout opens. You can [start free](https://sandbox.botstacks.ai/). New paid upgrades and add-ons are currently unavailable. Existing customers continue under their accepted subscription, support terms, billing dates and purchased balances. Your account and invoice show your current plan.
:::

## Credits, pages, and storage are different

- **AI credits** measure AI work. One credit is not one message and is not one provider token. The model, input, output, and memory work affect consumption.
- **Page units** measure successfully learned content. A page unit covers up to 8,000 normalized characters in a document. A longer document can use several units.
- **Knowledge sources** count retained source entries. One source can contain many pages.
- **Storage** measures retained content and vectors. Both limits apply. Deleting content frees storage; it does not restore already-consumed page units.

Model-specific AI rates will be published before paid usage opens. Do not convert old token balances into credits yourself or assume an automatic conversion.

## Renewals, balances, and taxes

For the upcoming prepaid offers, included monthly allowances reset each paid service period; unused included allowances do not roll over. Purchased usage does not expire. Pro and Growth share these pools within their workspace. Agency shares them across its organization, subject to each client’s limit. See [prepaid usage and limits](/billing/pay-as-you-go) for details.

Automatic refill is off by default. The upcoming model does not use postpaid usage overages. No new automatic-refill feature is available yet.

BotStacks does not collect taxes. Payments are processed through Stripe; the checkout or invoice shows the actual amount for an available purchase. If an invoice or checkout unexpectedly shows tax or a price you do not recognize, contact support before making another payment.

The new catalog uses monthly billing. Previously published $12 Pro, $19 Partners, Teams and Pro+ offers are legacy offers. Existing monthly and annual agreements keep their accepted terms. Use [subscription management](/billing/manage-subscription) for an existing account or the [public pricing page](https://www.botstacks.ai/pricing/) to compare options.
