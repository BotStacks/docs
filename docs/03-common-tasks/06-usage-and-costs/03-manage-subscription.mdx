# Manage Your Subscription

Use your existing subscription and invoice to identify your current price and allowances. The [upcoming catalog](/common-tasks/usage-and-costs/track-costs) does not replace an existing agreement automatically.

:::note Service availability
New paid upgrades and prepaid top-ups are currently unavailable. If account billing does not load, contact support instead of starting repeated checkouts. Do not assume a failed page load means a payment failed or a subscription was canceled.
:::

## Find your workspace billing

1. [Sign in to BotStacks](https://sandbox.botstacks.ai/login) and select the workspace you want to manage.
2. Open **Settings → Billing**. Some older interfaces show Billing directly in the sidebar.
3. Review the current subscription, renewal or scheduled cancellation, and invoice history available for that workspace.

Billing access depends on your workspace role. If billing controls are missing or you receive an access error, ask the workspace owner or administrator for help. An access error is not evidence that the workspace has no invoices.

## Payment methods and invoices

Where available, **Manage billing** opens the Stripe billing portal for the selected workspace. Use that portal to manage the payment method and access the billing documents it offers. Invoices can also appear in the workspace's billing history.

BotStacks does not collect taxes. For an unexpected charge or tax line, send support the invoice number and workspace name. Never send card security codes, full card numbers, passwords, or secret keys.

## Cancel or resume a subscription

For an existing subscription with cancellation controls, follow the confirmation in Billing. A scheduled cancellation ends the membership at the close of its paid billing period; it does not immediately end that paid period. Check the effective date shown in your account.

If a resume option is available before cancellation takes effect, use it to keep the existing subscription active. If no option appears, or the displayed dates disagree with your invoice, contact support. Do not open another subscription as a workaround.

Removing or archiving an assistant is not the same as canceling a paid subscription or removing a recurring add-on. Confirm the actual billing change and its effective date. Existing annual agreements keep their original terms; a new annual discount is not currently offered.

## Paid, but the plan has not updated?

1. Keep your Stripe receipt or invoice number.
2. Return to the same workspace used for checkout and reload Billing once.
3. If the plan still does not match the payment, contact [support@botstacks.ai](mailto:support@botstacks.ai). Include the workspace name, invoice or checkout reference, payment time, and the error message.
4. **Do not repeat the purchase while the first payment is being checked.** A successful payment and an updated workspace can be delayed separately.

Closing a checkout tab, seeing an error, or returning to the website is not proof that no charge occurred. Check the receipt or billing history first.

## Refunds, failed payments, and legacy plans

For duplicate charges, failed payments, refunds, or unfamiliar legacy plan names, contact support. Your current agreement determines your existing terms. Older screenshots and historical release notes do not establish the price of a new purchase.
