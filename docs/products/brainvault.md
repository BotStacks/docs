BrainVault is the knowledge area of BotStacks. In the app, open **Knowledge** and select the assistant you intend to work on. Source content and assistant instructions serve different purposes: content supplies facts; instructions guide how to respond.

## Choose the right source
| Source | Good use | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website URL | Public service and policy pages | Content was learned successfully |
| Text | A concise FAQ or approved correction | Wording is accurate and current |
| File | An approved reference document | The upload controls accept its type and size |

File types and size limits come from your account's upload configuration. Use the displayed limits; older screenshots do not establish current allowances.

## Assign and review sources
Confirm the selected assistant and the sources associated with it. Workspace visibility is not a guarantee that every assistant uses every source. Review processing status, titles, and the source contents before testing.

Use names that identify purpose and owner, such as “Returns policy — retail.” Avoid storing two conflicting versions. A descriptive title helps your team manage content; it is not a manual ranking control.

## Correct an answer
Find the missing or incorrect source. Update the approved fact, save it, wait for processing, and repeat the same question in DIRTbox. Also test a paraphrase and an unrelated question to catch regressions.

Deleting content changes retained storage and may change future answers. It does not undo usage already consumed. Do not delete the only current policy source just to hide a bad answer.

## Protect private information
Keep passwords, payment details, and private visitor records out of website-assistant knowledge. Check source assignment before making an assistant available to visitors.
