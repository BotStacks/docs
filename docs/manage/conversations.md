Open **Chat History** to review conversations available to your workspace. The website calls this capability **Conversation Insights**; the current app navigation uses Chat History.

## Review with a purpose
Look for repeated unanswered questions, inaccurate claims, confusing replies, and failed next steps. Separate a source-content problem from an installation or service error. Do not infer that the assistant is healthy just because no new conversation is visible.

## Turn findings into improvements
1. Record a redacted example and the expected response.
2. Locate the approved source that should support the answer.
3. Correct knowledge or assistant behavior.
4. Save a representative test in DIRTbox where available.
5. Re-test before publishing the change.

## Respect access and retention
Available history depends on workspace access and retention terms. Missing history is not proof that no conversation happened. Use [billing guidance](/common-tasks/usage-and-costs/track-costs) for current versus upcoming retention allowances.

Do not copy visitor personal information into public docs, test fixtures, or support screenshots. Use minimal, redacted examples. For privacy requests, contact [support](/resources/contact-support).
